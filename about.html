I grew up drawing, so graphic design was an easy choice. While I was still at university, I started designing apps for real clients and taught myself Figma along the way. That turned into a career in UI/UX, and later into my own studio, TL Studio. Running it taught me that good design is only half the job. The other half is explaining it to clients. Now I want to bring that to a bigger team.

When work gets stressful, I go to the gym and lift something heavy. The rest of my free time goes to psychology and whatever I’m curious about this week. I’m an INTP, so there’s always something.
